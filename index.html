Score360 (“the app”, “we”) is a football predictions and tips app operated by [LEGAL ENTITY NAME], Nairobi, Kenya. 2. Why we use it

  • To run the app — keep your favourites and settings, show your feed, deliver the notifications you chose.
  • Community features — display the tips and slips you share, and let others follow you.
  • Premium subscription — unlock premium content after a purchase and keep it unlocked across devices.
  • Safety — act on content reports, honour user blocks, prevent abuse and keep the service available.
  • Accuracy statistics — we measure how often the app’s statistical predictions are right. This uses match results, not your data.

Legal bases (where GDPR applies): performance of our contract with you, our legitimate interests in running a safe service, and your consent for notifications and sign-in.

4. How long we keep it

  • Your account and content — until you delete your account (see section 6). Deletion is immediate and permanent.
  • Guest accounts that have never saved anything and have been inactive for 90 days are deleted automatically.
  • Push tokens are removed as soon as a device stops accepting notifications or you uninstall.
  • Payment records (order references, M-Pesa transaction ids) are kept as long as accounting law requires, then deleted.
  • Technical logs — a few days.

5. Your rights

You can, at any time:

  • Export a copy of your data — email us (section 9) and you receive a machine-readable file with your profile, favourites, tips, slips and search history.
  • Correct your profile details.
  • Turn off any notification category, or all notifications in your device settings.
  • Delete your account and all its data (section 6).

If you are in the EU/EEA, UK or Kenya you also have the rights to object, to restrict processing and to complain to your data-protection authority (in Kenya, the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner). To exercise any right not available in the app, contact us at the address below.

6. Delete your account

You can delete your Score360 account yourself, without contacting us:

  1. Open the app and go to Profile → Account.
  2. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

This permanently removes your account, profile, favourites, saved matches, tips, comments, slips, advisor answers, push tokens and search history. It cannot be undone.

Subscriptions: deleting your account does not cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription — cancel it in your Apple ID or Google Play subscription settings, otherwise you may continue to be billed.

If you no longer have the app installed, email [SUPPORT EMAIL] from the address linked to your account, or with your display name, and we will delete it within 30 days.

7. Age

Score360 is intended for adults. You must be 18 or older to use it. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18; if you believe we have, contact us and we will delete it.

This privacy policy applies to the Score360 app (“the app”), published and operated by LiveGame Score (“we”, “us”). It explains what personal and sensitive user data the app accesses, collects, uses and shares; how we handle it securely; how long we keep it; and how you can delete it.

Score360 is a football predictions and tips app. It offers no real-money gambling: it shows statistical predictions, community tips and shared tip slips, and sells an optional premium content subscription. It does not take bets or make payouts.

2. How we use it

  • App functionality — keep your favourites and settings, show your feed, deliver the notifications you chose, unlock premium after a purchase.
  • Community features — display the tips and slips you share, and let others follow you.
  • Safety, security and fraud prevention — act on content reports, honour user blocks, prevent abuse, keep the service available.
  • Analytics and bug fixing — aggregate usage statistics and crash reports.
  • Advertising — show ads to free users, which funds the free tier.

We do not use your data for any purpose not listed here. Where GDPR or similar law applies, our legal bases are: performance of our contract with you; our legitimate interests in running a safe, working service; and your consent for notifications, Google sign-in and personalised advertising, which you can withdraw at any time.

Advertising

Free users may see ads served by a third-party advertising network, which may use your device’s advertising ID to show ads that are more relevant to you and to measure them. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads at any time in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking). Premium subscribers see no ads.

4. How we protect it

  • All data sent between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit using TLS.
  • Every database table is protected by row-level access rules, so an account can only read or change its own data; server-side access uses restricted credentials.
  • Passwords are never stored in readable form — only as salted hashes. With Google sign-in we never receive a password at all.
  • Payments are handled by the app stores and payment processors — we never see card numbers.
  • Access to production systems is limited to the people who operate them. Backups are kept in the same restricted environment.

If we become aware of a breach affecting your data we will notify you and the relevant authorities as the law requires.

6. Your rights and choices

  • Access / export — email us (section 10) for a machine-readable copy of your profile, favourites, tips, slips and search history.
  • Correct — edit your profile in the app.
  • Notifications — switch any category off in the app, or all notifications in your device settings.
  • Advertising — see “Advertising” in section 3.
  • Delete — delete your account and its data yourself (section 7).

Depending on where you live you may also have the right to object to or restrict processing, to data portability, and to complain to your local data-protection authority. We answer requests within 30 days, free of charge.

7. Delete your account

You can delete your Score360 account and its data yourself, at any time:

  1. Open the app and go to Profile → Account.
  2. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

This permanently deletes your account, profile, avatar, favourites, saved matches, tips, comments, slips, advisor answers, push tokens and search history. It cannot be undone. Only payment records are retained, as described in section 5.

If you no longer have the app, request deletion on our account deletion page.

Subscriptions: deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play or App Store subscription — cancel it in your Google Play or Apple ID subscription settings, or you may continue to be billed.

8. Age

Score360 is for adults. You must be 18 or older to use it, and it is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18; if you believe we have, contact us and we will delete it.

Delete your Score360 account

Score360 by LiveGame Score  ·  Privacy Policy  ·  Support

This page explains how to delete your Score360 account and the data associated with it, with or without the app installed.

Option 1 — in the app (immediate)

  1. Open Score360.
  2. Go to Profile → Account.
  3. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

Your account and data are deleted immediately.

Option 2 — without the app

Send an email to livegamescore@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my Score360 account”, from the email address of your Score360 account (the one you signed in with, by Google, by Apple or by email). If you used Score360 as a guest without an account, or signed in with Apple using “Hide My Email”, include your display name.

We confirm the request by replying to that address and delete the account within 30 days (usually within 2 working days).

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play or App Store subscription. Cancel it first so you are not billed again: Google Play · App Store.

Delete some data without deleting the account

You can remove individual tips, comments and slips, clear your search history, and turn off notifications in the app at any time. For anything else, email us at the address above.
